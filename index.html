AI Cloud Storage System Survey

A two-book survey. Book I · AI: how cloud service providers (CSPs), inference engines and storage vendors manage the LLM KV cache across HBM, DRAM, SSD, remote pools and HDD — and whether HDD can serve as the bottom tier. Book II · Big data: how EB-scale storage systems optimize HDD I/O, what limits the use of high-capacity, high-density HDDs, and under what conditions larger drives deliver real value.

Part 1 · CSP and vendor survey →15 vendor chapters, each with a KV placement-strategy section and a hardware-configuration table, plus a cross-vendor placement comparison (Chinese). Part 2 · AI inference engines →SGLang, vLLM, DeepInfer (DeepSeek): KV cache management implementation with block diagrams, HDD as the bottom tier, and how each engine maps onto concrete hardware (accelerators, interconnects, NICs, SSD, HDD) with worked sizing examples (Chinese). Part 3 · Big-data I/O optimization →9 chapters: AWS S3, Google Colossus, Meta Tectonic, Azure, Alibaba Pangu / OSS, Chinese vendors and policy, HDFS / Ozone / Ceph, Dropbox / Backblaze, HDD vendor technology (Chinese). Part 4 · HDD density and capacity →6 chapters: IOPS/TB, rebuild and reliability, SMR / zoned write model, TCO; what drives the preference for mid-capacity HDDs in some Chinese big-data deployments; recommendations (Chinese). Report (PDF) →281-page PDF edition covering both books, with a table of contents page and bookmark outline (~88 MB). Knowledge Base →Markdown chapters, topic notes, extracted figures and a source index of 600+ references (Chinese).
